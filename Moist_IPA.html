Welcome Aboard

When the sails are lowered, the golden hour hits the water, and the groove of a smooth bassline drifts across the deck, there is only one beverage meant for your hand.

Brewed exclusively by Diamond Rock Monument Brewing Co., Moist IPA is a perfectly balanced, super drinkable companion for the sophisticated voyager. It goes down as effortlessly as a warm ocean breeze.

Tasting Notes for the Discerning Sailor

Expect a refreshing, perfectly calibrated hop profile that doesn't overwhelm the palate, ensuring you stay steady at the helm.

It’s light enough for a sun-drenched afternoon on the bow, yet complex enough to savor as the sun dips below the horizon. Whether navigating the high seas or dropping anchor in your own backyard, every bottle is an invitation to kick back.

Chilled by the Tide

Let the ocean chill your bottle and the music warm your soul. Slip off your deck shoes, wade into the shallows, and taste the sophistication of smooth sailing.

When the compass points to leisure, Moist IPA is the only provision you need. Crafted for those who appreciate the rhythm of the waves and the undeniable groove of a smooth saxophone solo, this IPA is best enjoyed where the water meets the sand.
